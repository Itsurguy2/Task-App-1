# Project 7 - *TASK APP 1*

Submitted by: **JESSE ROSENTHAL**

**Task Schedule app** The Task App is an app that helps you organize and manage your daily tasks with a beautiful, modern interface. Create new tasks with titles, optional notes, 
and due dates, then track your progress as you complete them. The app features two main views: a task list that shows all your tasks sorted by completion status and creation date, 
and a calendar view that organizes tasks by their due dates. 

Key features include:
- **Smart Task Management**: Add, edit, and delete tasks with titles, notes, and due dates
- **Completion Tracking**: Tap the completion button to mark tasks as done with satisfying animations and haptic feedback
- **Intuitive Organization**: Incomplete tasks appear first, followed by completed ones sorted by completion date
- **Calendar View**: See your tasks organized by due date for better planning
- **Swipe to Delete**: Easily remove tasks you no longer need
- **Professional Design**: Modern card-based layout with beautiful colors, gradients, and smooth animations
- **Dark Mode Support**: Automatically adapts to your device's appearance settings
- **Persistent Storage**: All tasks are automatically saved and restored when you reopen the app

The app combines powerful functionality with an elegant design, making task management both efficient and enjoyable. Whether you're planning your day or organizing long-term 
projects, the Task App provides a clean, distraction-free experience that helps you stay productive.
Time spent: **11** hours spent in total
